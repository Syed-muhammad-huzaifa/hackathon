# Data Model: Physical AI Explanation

This feature focuses on generating educational content. The "data model" here refers to the conceptual entities being explained within the textbook section, rather than a software data model for persistence or API interactions.

## Conceptual Entities

### Physical AI
-   **Description**: An artificial intelligence system that interacts with the physical world through a body, sensors, and actuators, making intelligent decisions based on real-time environmental data and physical laws.
-   **Key Characteristics**:
    -   Requires a physical embodiment (body).
    -   Interacts with and perceives the physical environment via sensors.
    -   Acts upon the physical environment via actuators.
    -   Decisions are influenced by real-world physics (e.g., gravity, friction, momentum).
    -   Examples: Drones, self-driving cars, warehouse robots, humanoids.
-   **Relationship to Robot**: Physical AI is the "brain" and intelligence driving a robot's physical interactions.

### Digital AI
-   **Description**: An artificial intelligence system that operates solely within digital environments, processing data, generating text, or playing games without direct interaction with the physical world.
-   **Key Characteristics**:
    -   No physical embodiment required.
    -   Interacts with digital data and virtual environments.
    -   Decisions are based on digital logic and algorithms.
    -   Examples: Chatbots, recommendation engines, chess programs.
-   **Relationship to Physical AI**: Contrasted with Physical AI to highlight the unique challenges and requirements of physical interaction.

### Robot
-   **Description**: A machine capable of carrying out a complex series of actions automatically, especially one programmable by computer. In the context of Physical AI, it refers to a machine with a physical body that interacts with its environment.
-   **Key Characteristics**:
    -   Physical body (hardware).
    -   Sensors to perceive the environment.
    -   Actuators to manipulate the environment.
    -   Often driven by a form of AI (which could be Digital AI for planning or Physical AI for direct physical interaction).
-   **Relationship to Physical AI**: The physical embodiment through which Physical AI manifests its intelligence and interacts with the real world.
