# Data Model: Digital AI vs Physical AI

This feature focuses on generating educational content. The "data model" here refers to the conceptual entities being explained within the textbook section, rather than a software data model for persistence or API interactions.

## Conceptual Entities

### Digital AI
-   **Description**: Artificial intelligence that operates purely within a software environment, processing data and executing logic without direct physical interaction.
-   **Key Characteristics**:
    -   Operates in a virtual space.
    -   Deals with data, algorithms, and logical operations.
    -   No physical embodiment.
    -   Examples: Chatbots (e.g., ChatGPT), recommendation systems, financial trading algorithms, image recognition software (without physical output).
-   **Relationship to Physical AI**: Contrasted with Physical AI to highlight interaction domains.

### Physical AI
-   **Description**: Artificial intelligence embedded in a physical system (like a robot) that interacts with and operates within the real world, experiencing physical phenomena.
-   **Key Characteristics**:
    -   Possesses a physical body (robot, drone, self-driving car).
    -   Interacts with the real world through sensors (perception) and actuators (action).
    -   Must account for physical laws (gravity, friction) and real-world uncertainties (dynamic environments, safety).
    -   Examples: Self-driving cars, industrial robots, humanoid robots, drones.
-   **Relationship to Digital AI**: Uses digital algorithms, but its intelligence is expressed through physical action in the real world.

### Virtual Space
-   **Description**: The non-physical, computational environment where Digital AI exists and operates. It is a controlled environment defined by software rules.
-   **Key Characteristics**:
    -   Governed by programmed logic.
    -   Absence of physical constraints like gravity or friction.
    -   Data is its primary medium.

### Real World
-   **Description**: The physical environment characterized by gravity, friction, dynamic objects, and safety considerations, where Physical AI operates. It is an unstructured and often unpredictable environment.
-   **Key Characteristics**:
    -   Governed by physical laws.
    -   Requires sensing and acting in a continuous, analog space.
    -   Involves safety considerations due to physical interaction.
